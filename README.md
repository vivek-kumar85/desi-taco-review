# The Desi Taco Co. review helper

The page customers open from the QR code on the counter at The Desi Taco Co., Salunkhe Vihar, Pune.

Customers pick their own star rating and what they had, liked or would improve. The page drafts a short review from those choices only, the customer edits it, then copies it and opens Google's review form to paste and post it themselves.

- Everything runs in the customer's browser. Nothing is sent or stored.
- One self-contained file: `index.html` (the logo is embedded).
- Google review link: https://g.page/r/CQxIrJg7w7Q-EAE/review
